SportsAgenticAgent has been deployed!

1. Forward the service port locally to access the API:
  kubectl --namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }} port-forward svc/{{ include "sportsagentic-agent.fullname" . }} 8000:{{ .Values.service.port }}

2. Check upstream health (MCP, llama-server, and Gemini):
  curl -s http://localhost:8000/health

3. Send a chat query:
  curl -s http://localhost:8000/chat \
    -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
    -d '{"message": "What is the schedule for Ole Miss?", "provider": "auto"}'
